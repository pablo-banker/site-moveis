# Proposta de imagem para o parallax

Imagem base: `sala-minimalista-base-v1.png`.

Proposta inicial para revisão da composição, gerada com a ferramenta integrada de imagens. Ainda não aplicada ao site.

## Separação prevista

1. Arquitetura e piso: fundo completo, reconstruído atrás dos móveis.
2. Luminária e mesa lateral: camada distante com transparência.
3. Sofá e almofadas: camada intermediária com transparência.
4. Tapete e mesa de centro: camada próxima com transparência.
5. Poltrona: primeiro plano com transparência.

As camadas devem compartilhar o tamanho de tela e as coordenadas da base. Reconstruir trechos ocultos para evitar buracos durante o movimento. A área vazia à esquerda acomoda o texto. Como o tapete encosta na borda direita, prever margem extra na separação e no enquadramento.

Para preservar texturas, usar PNGs com alfa; SVGs servem para uma futura versão vetorial redesenhada. A intensidade do scroll será menor no fundo e maior no primeiro plano.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: full base concept image for a layered scroll parallax hero background of Forma, a minimalist furniture ecommerce website.
Create one complete, polished wide landscape scene, approximately 16:9. A serene contemporary living room with rounded furniture, in white, light gray and graphite. Elegant architectural visualization with tactile matte materials, gently stylized yet convincing dimensionality. No interface, no text, no logos.
Composition: camera straight-on with slight elevation, consistent perspective. Keep the left 40 percent mostly quiet pale wall and floor for website copy. Concentrate the furniture composition in the right 60 percent. Broad off-white walls, an understated recessed arch and soft daylight form the distant background. In the middle distance, a complete rounded graphite sofa with light gray cushions, a slim curved floor lamp, and a small pale side table. In front, an oval light-gray rug and a low rounded charcoal coffee table, plus a light gray rounded armchair toward the right foreground. Furniture silhouettes must be clean, distinct, fully visible, with generous separation and room around all edges.
Parallax preparation: make clear depth separation between architecture, rear furnishing, sofa, and foreground furniture; no large object cropped off the image. Simple floor, subtle realistic contact shadows, no reflections, no curtains crossing objects, no tangled plants or tiny decorations. This image will later be split into registered transparent layers with the same camera and canvas. Preserve easily reconstructible wall and floor behind each object.
Lighting: soft daylight from upper left, sophisticated calm premium atmosphere. Restrained white/gray/graphite palette, no colorful accents, no people. High quality furniture forms, refined rounded edges, no excessive ornamentation. Deliver the complete assembled scene, opaque background.
